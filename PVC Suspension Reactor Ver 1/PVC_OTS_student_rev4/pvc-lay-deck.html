Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Read the north arrow, EL and written lengths; never scale.
  • The BOM counts pipe, fittings, valves and supports from the geometry.
  • L-113 and L-115 fall all the way; L-103 may climb.
  • Twelve stations cover all 17 hand valves and every local gauge.
