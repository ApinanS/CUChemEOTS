Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Each line meets its service criterion: all 15 pass.
  • L-103: 6 in, 2.97 m/s, 39.5 kPa per 100 m.
  • Vapour lines grow as pressure falls: 6 in, then 12 in.
  • P-103 NPSHa 3.83 m vs 3.5 m: no room for heat gain.

Key Takeaways

1

Write a batch HMB step by step, and close it before anyone designs to it.

2

Size each item at its own moment of the batch, not at one design point.

3

Check the cooling margin at the hottest cooling water the site will see.

4

Judge every line by its service, and size vapour lines at their lowest pressure.

5

Keep the VCM suction short and cool: saturated storage leaves almost no NPSH margin.
