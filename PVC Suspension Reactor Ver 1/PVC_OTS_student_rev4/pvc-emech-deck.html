The jacketed wall is loaded from both sides

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Internal pressure gives 30 mm shell, 35 mm heads.
  • Jacket 5.2 bar g plus vacuum: 6.21 bar from outside.
  • Buckling governs: 40 mm shell, 38 mm heads.
  • Stiffening rings shorten $L$ and recover most of the extra wall.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • M-101 absorbs 290 kW at 2.0 h, 338 kW at the end.

  • P-103 at shut-off can exceed the R-101 design.

Key Takeaways

1

Tag every value in the equipment list with its source, and chase the assumed ones.

2

Set design pressure above the highest credible point and the PSV set.

3

Check a jacketed shell for external pressure before trusting the internal result.

4

Rate motors on the batch peak, not on the model's base constant.

5

Design the jacket for its own pump's shut-off pressure.
