Suspension polymerisation

Each VCM droplet is a small bulk reactor

  • The agitator disperses VCM; PVA holds the droplets apart.
  • The initiator dissolves in the droplet: bulk polymerisation inside.
  • PVC precipitates as a gel swollen with VCM.
  • Water carries the heat to the cooled walls.

What part 1 settled

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • At the peak: jacket 40 %, baffles 11 %, E-101 48 % of the heat.
  • The jacket is UA-limited; E-101 adds area off the vessel wall.
  • Inerts blanket E-101; they must be vented.
  • R-101 is open-loop unstable: the temperature loop must be reliable.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Recipe values first, running values held, pressure watched.
  • The initiator goes in last; then the heat curve is fixed.
  • Cooling caps the initiator dose and the warm-day batch.
  • Size from capacity, then check cooling at the peak.

This deck

Key Takeaways

1

Size heat removal on the peak release, never on the average.

2

Treat reaction temperature as the grade specification, not a speed handle.

3

Check what limits each sink: UA for the jacket, inerts for E-101.

4

Compare the generation and removal slopes before trusting any operating point.

5

Verify the cooling margin with every handle wide open at the peak.
