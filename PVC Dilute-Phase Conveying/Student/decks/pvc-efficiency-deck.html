More air, more margin, more energy

At 0.78 kg/s the lowest point reaches saltation; at 1.20 the blower is at 100 % speed.

The target must cover the variation

Halve σ and the target can drop from 1.2 toward 1.1: steady operation pays in energy.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • +1 % air costs about +1.9 % energy.
  • The margin target sets the air, and the air sets the energy.
  • Lower variation, not a bolder target, buys the saving.

Does cooler conveying air save energy?

A rule of thumb says cold air conveys cheaper. The model can check it.

From 50 to 30 °C the energy falls by less than 1 %: not worth a bigger cooler.

Interactive · savings per year from the model

What is each option worth?

The VFD and the stepped bore already save the most; of the new options, the air schedule leads at part load.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Cooler air saves under 1 %; the silo filter about 1.8 % per kPa.
  • An air schedule pays most when the unit runs below design.
  • Design choices (VFD, stepped bore) saved more than any later option.

Key Takeaways

  • Measure kWh per tonne, not kW.
  • Trim air only as far as the variation allows.
  • Test every rule of thumb on the model before spending money.
