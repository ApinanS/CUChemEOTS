Interactive · scripted cold startups on the model, trips live

How fast should the feed come up?

Step 9 said ramp the feed. The model shows how fast.

v/vsaltfeed ÷ 10, t/h

A step dips v/vsalt to 1.02; 6 t/h per min holds 1.2 and reaches 18 t/h in about 3 minutes.

Interactive · the same runs as the tab

Which moves first: air or feed?

Up: air first keeps the margin. Down: air first drops it below 1.0 and leaves 1.1 kg of resin; feed first is right.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Ramp the feed at 6 t/h per minute or slower.
  • Going up: air first, then feed.
  • Going down: feed first, then air.

Interactive · from a line run 15 min at 0.74 kg/s (243 kg of dunes)

How long must the purge run?

About 60 s at 0.98 kg/s clears the dunes; the procedure asks for 2 minutes.

Interactive · restart runs of the tab

Blower trip with resin in the line

A purge empties a running line. After a trip the resin is already lying in it.

The line clears on restart if the air comes back full; a changeover costs about 4 minutes and 0.8 t.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Purge at full air for at least 2 minutes before the blower stops.
  • After a trip, restart at full air before any feed.
  • Change silos only with the line empty.

This deck

Key Takeaways

  • Start air first and stop it last.
  • Ramp the feed; never step it into a running line.
  • Leave the line empty at every stop.
