Layers of protection

Prevent first, then mitigate

HAZOP listed safeguards row by row. Here they are sorted by how they act.

Prevention · layers 1–3

Control (BPCS), alarms with operator response, and the safety instrumented system (SIS) keep the deviation from becoming a release.

Mitigation · layers 4–6

Relief and venting, containment, and emergency response limit the harm once it happens.

Independence

A layer counts only if it fails on its own

The ladders look right. Next: do the rungs share a sensor?

Shared

PIC-102, PAH-102 and PSHH-102 all read PT-102. A plugged impulse line blinds all three.

Independent

PAH-101 reads PI-101; PSV-101 is mechanical. Neither depends on PT-102.

What part 3 settled

Key Takeaways

  • Place every safeguard in one layer, with its setting.
  • Order the settings: control, alarm, trip, relief, limit.
  • Give each credited layer its own sensor.
