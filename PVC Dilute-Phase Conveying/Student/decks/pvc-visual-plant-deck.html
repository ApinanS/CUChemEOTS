Interactive · rounds sheet, normal values from a design snapshot

What to read at each station

Normal values are the design state; on a round the sheet is checked against the plant as it runs.

Interactive · tolerance as a share of the normal value

How close is close enough?

A local gauge has a coarse dial; the sheet allows for that.

Tolerances are a few percent: enough for a dial, tight enough to catch a real change.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • The sheet lists 28 items with units and tolerances.
  • Most gauges are at grade; levels and filter ΔP are on the roofs.
  • A reading outside tolerance is a finding, not a mistake.

Interactive · pick a tag, find it on the picture

Which item is which?

Before reading or operating anything, the operator must find the right item.

Tags on the plant match the P&ID: the tag, not the shape, identifies an item.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Nine hand valves normally open or closed, two locked open.
  • Every valve is operable from grade or a platform.
  • Identify by tag before you read or operate.

This deck

Key Takeaways

  • Walk the round in a fixed order and log every reading.
  • Compare each reading with its normal value and tolerance.
  • Know the normal state of every hand valve.
