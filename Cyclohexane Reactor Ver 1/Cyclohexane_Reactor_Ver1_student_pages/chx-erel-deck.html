Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • $A = W/(C K_d P_1 K_b K_c)\sqrt{TZ/M}$ for critical gas flow.
  • Orifices: PSV-101 Q, PSV-102 K, PSV-104 J, PSV-112 J, PSV-109 P.
  • Fire: wetted area within 7.6 m; the plot plan sets it.
  • Inlet loss < 3 %, back pressure < 10 % of set.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Contingencies add loads that arrive together; 178.7 t/h governs.
  • A 30" header, 176 m, keeps back pressure at 1.31 bar a.
  • EDV-101 must reach 6.9 bar g in 15 min.
  • Liquid flash sets the orifice: ≈ 1 756 mm², gas cools to −55 °C.

Key Takeaways

1

Ask every scenario; the largest required area governs.

2

Give no credit to controllers or trips in a relief case.

3

Size with $A = W/(C K_d P_1 K_b K_c)\sqrt{TZ/M}$, then round up a letter.

4

Keep inlet loss below 3 % and back pressure below 10 %.

5

Size the flare for simultaneous loads, the EDV with liquid flash.
