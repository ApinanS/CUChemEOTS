Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • PSM requires written procedures for every operating phase.
  • One format: purpose, hazards, prerequisites, steps, abnormal, sign-off.
  • Each step: action, expected response, reason; holds and cautions marked.
  • SOPs are verified, approved, trained, used and reviewed yearly.

Interactive · SOP-02 on the model's reference run

Cold start-up: action, expected response, reason

SOP-01 leaves D-400 empty at 30 kPa g N2; the start-up begins from that state.

Outlets open, then pressure, feed, heat; N2 out, liquid seal, level, full pressure; rate last: lined out in about 83 min.

Interactive · SOP-02 step 5, model runs at 8 m³/h

Why TCV-401 opens only 15–20 % by hand

TIC-401 goes to AUTO at 62 °C; it then needs only ≈ 13 % at 8 m³/h.

A bigger hand opening warms faster but 35 % overshoots to 84 °C, past the 79 °C alarm: the step carries a CAUTION.

Interactive · SOP-03, click the six actions in order

Shutdown order: what goes first?

The way down is not the way up reversed: the heat must follow the feed at once.

    Feed, then heat at once, then pressure, level, drain and isolation: each step removes what the next one would fight.

    Interactive · SOP-03 step 3, model runs

    Why the heat goes off with the feed

    Bubble pressure, Raoult's lawPbub​=xPC4sat​(T)+(1−x)PC6sat​(T)

    Trapped feed heated by hot oil boils against closed valves: 3 min late gives 124 °C, 1175 kPa g, far above the 650 kPa g supply.

    Interactive · SOP-03 step 4 and SOP-11

    Depressure at a rate the header can take

    PCV-420 opened 5 % every 30 s keeps the surge below the 900 Nm³/h alarm; the drain that follows is attended because flashing liquid chills.

    Part 2 · summary

    What part 2 settled

    • Six N2 cycles, 10 → 100 kPa g, bring O2 below 1 %.
    • Start-up: outlets, pressure, feed, heat, seal, level, rate last.
    • Warm the feed by hand at 15–20 %, then TIC-401 to AUTO.
    • Shutdown: feed off, heat off at once, slow depressure, attended drain.

    Interactive · SOP-04, model runs with no operator action

    How long before the SIS acts?

    Start-up and shutdown are planned; an upset gives only the minutes between alarm and trip.

    The alarm comes minutes before the trip: that gap is the time the SOP response has, and the reset waits for the permissives.

    Interactive · SOP-07, steady-state model gains near design

    Which handle, and how far?

    TIC-401 is the strong handle and it moves both products the same way: one spec is always traded against the other.

      Part 3 · summary

      What part 3 settled

      • Trips come minutes after alarms; restart only through the permissives.
      • TIC-401 is the strong spec handle; wait about 18 min per move.
      • By hand, level moves 0.28 %/min per m³/h: small steps.
      • Isolate every source, bypasses included, then prove zero energy.

      Key Takeaways

      1

      Write one action, one expected response and one reason per step.

      3

      4
