Definitions

A deviation is a guide word applied to a parameter

A team, not one engineer, walks every section of D-400 with every guide word.

Design intent

E-400 must heat the feed to °C and keep it liquid.

Deviation

MORE + temperature → hot feed above set point.

What it finds

Hazards to people and plant; operability problems such as off-spec product.

Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Deviation = guide word × parameter of a stated design intent.
  • IEC 61882: four phases; examination runs node → parameter → guide word.
  • Every meaningful deviation is recorded, safe or not.
  • Severity ignores safeguards; likelihood includes them.

After the meeting

Follow-up and close-out

  1. Every recommendation gets an owner, a date, a response.
  2. High rows relying on SIS I-1 or I-2 go to LOPA.
  3. Relief cases go to the PSV-400 study; changes to MOC.
  4. Revalidate about every five years; train on the runs.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Risk = S × L; most D-400 rows are Medium.
  • High rows lean on trips or relief and go to LOPA.
  • The model shows how long safeguards take, and what never trips.
  • Close-out: owners, relief checks, MOC, revalidation.

This deck

Key Takeaways

  1. State each node's design intent with numbers before applying guide words.
  2. Judge severity without safeguards and likelihood with them.
  3. Write safeguards as real tags, limits and fail positions.
  4. Send rows that rely on trips to LOPA.
  5. Run deviations on the model to see how much time the operator has.
