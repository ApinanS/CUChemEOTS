Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Gauges, hand valves and condition are field-only.
  • Local indicators give the board a second opinion.
  • LT-400 depends on density; LG-400 does not.
  • Every hand valve has a normal position to check.

NC · normally closed

Globe bypass

HV-403, HV-413, HV-423: closed; throttle on board request.

LO · locked open

Six station blocks: car seal, permit only.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Twelve stations, about 115 m, grade to EL 108.000.
  • Each station has items to read, check or look at.
  • Control-valve stations: blocks open, bypasses shut.
  • Most readings are at grade; four floors in all.

Key Takeaways

1

Walk the round for what no transmitter sends: gauges, hand valves, condition.

2

Compare each local reading with its board value; report any gap.

3

Check every hand valve against its normal position: NO, NC or LO.

4

Treat LT-400 with care when the liquid is not the design liquid.

5

Write each reading on the sheet where you take it.
