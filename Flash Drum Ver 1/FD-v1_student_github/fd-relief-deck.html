Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Loads are sized at 596.3 kPa a, or 645.8 kPa a for fire.
  • A blocked outlet relieves only with a feed hotter than normal.
  • FCV-400 wide open: PCV-420 passes the vapour, no lift.
  • External fire governs: 4 247 kg/h, J orifice.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • The model confirms: no relief with the feed at 74 °C.
  • PSHH-426 would trip 400–1 000 s before any lift.
