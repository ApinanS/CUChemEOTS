What part 3 settled

  • A bottom-nozzle pool fire engulfs D-400 itself.
  • D-400 burst: about 10 kg TNT; the fireball reaches the building.

This deck

Key Takeaways

1

Hand one number down the chain: rate, distance, effect, people, severity.

2

Bound every hole equation by what the plant can supply and isolate.

3

Check the occupied building against the cloud, the blast and the fireball.

4

Treat a severity that differs from HAZID or LOPA as a question for the team.

5

Trust a screening engine only after it reproduces its golden cases.
