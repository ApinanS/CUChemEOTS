Key Takeaways

1

Lay out in process order, then check every distance against a rule.

2

Raise a query when a fixed position fails; do not move it quietly.

3

Put every instrument a person must read or test within reach.

4

Check the static head of a liquid at its bubble point at low level and high flow.

5

Write the geometry once, in layout.json, and draw everything from it.
