What part 1 settled

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Flow → FCV-400, temperature → TCV-401, level → LCV-400, pressure → PCV-420.
  • A gain that changes sign rules a pairing out.
  • T and P stand in for composition: fast and reliable.
  • Cascade cures valve-path upsets; feedforward cures measured loads.
  • The SIS stays separate, on its own sensors.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • P alone leaves e0/(1 + KcK); PI removes it.
  • One 5 % bump in MAN gives K, τ and θ.
  • Lambda PI: Kc = τ/[K(λ + θ)], Ti = τ.
  • Gain and dead time grow at low feed: retune or detune.
  • Level is averaging: slow outflow, not tight level.

This deck

Key Takeaways

1

Rank the objectives, then count the valves before drawing a loop.

2

Pair each CV with the valve that moves it most, fastest, with one sign.

3

Hold T and P for quality; let the analysers tell you when to move them.

4

Add cascade for valve-path upsets and feedforward for measured loads.

5

Tune from a step test on the model, and keep the level averaging.
