Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Four valves hold four variables: n = m = 4.
  • Level is an inventory; temperature and pressure carry quality.
  • Both quality handles push both specs the same way.
  • λ₁₁ ≈ 1.4: pair AI-420 with TIC-401, AI-410 with PIC-420.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Valve flexibility is the pressure it may take.
  • Feed: the boiling margin binds before FCV-400 travel.
  • Vapour: the header pressure, not PCV-420 size, limits.
  • Heat: TCV-401 has about 40 % spare at design.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • FIC-400 alone sets the rate; the rest follows.
  • Cascades trade valve travel for smaller swings.
  • FCV-400 is nearest 95 % as the rate rises.
  • Feed composition needs set-point moves, not more travel.

Key Takeaways

1

Count handles against held variables; inventories add no quality freedom.

2

Check the gains: parallel handles need large, cancelling moves.

3

Find the pressure or temperature that binds before the valve does.

4

Keep one rate handle and let flow slaves use the spare travel.
