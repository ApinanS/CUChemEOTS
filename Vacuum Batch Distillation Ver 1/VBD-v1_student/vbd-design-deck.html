Rayleigh balance of the pot

The scrub showed drift; a mole balance on the pot explains it.

Derivation · stages at total reflux

Fenske: the fewest stages for a split

$\alpha_{ij}$
relative volatility of light key i to heavy key j
–
$x_D,\ x_B$

–
$N_{min}$
theoretical stages at total reflux
stages
$\alpha_{ij}=P^{sat}_i/P^{sat}_j$ideal liquid, both at the same T
$N_{min}=\dfrac{\ln\!\big[\frac{x_D}{1-x_D}\cdot\frac{1-x_B}{x_B}\big]}{\ln\alpha_{ij}}$one factor α per stage at total reflux

A finite reflux needs more stages: keep N ≥ 1.5 Nmin (rule of thumb).

Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Constant R plus a shrinking pot: cut points end each cut.
  • Pressure sets the temperatures; the residue's bubble point limits it.
  • Q sets V; R sets D; m/D sets the cut times.
  • C16/C18 needs about 12 stages at total reflux; 20 are installed.
  • Flooding, not heating, caps the duty.

Assumptions behind the sizing

  • α and λ: model vapour pressures, ΔHv assumed.
  • Fmax 2.8 Pa0.5: typical packing, not a vendor rating.
  • Off-cut 55 % lights and 80 % recovery: from the reference campaign.
  • Times exclude heat-up, about 100 min at 8 kW.
  • Short-cut: constant boil-up and cut composition.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Batch, constant R, packing, tempered water, hot oil with duty control.
  • Spec → pressure → stages → duty → utilities → control.
  • Short-cut CAP 2.44 kg/h vs 2.29 kg/h on the model.
  • A pilot-like 53 mbar needs a wider column.

Key Takeaways

  • Set the pressure by the residue's bubble point at the end of the campaign.
  • Size the duty by flooding; the rates and cut times follow from Q and R.
  • Check stages on the hardest split, C16/C18, with a 1.5 × margin.
  • Start cooling and vacuum before heat, every campaign.
  • Test short-cut numbers against the model before trusting them.
