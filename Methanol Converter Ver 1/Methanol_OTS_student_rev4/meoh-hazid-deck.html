Reaction and catalyst

208 t of copper catalyst is a hazard at every stage

Part 1 found the hazards by area. Two of the loop's own materials, catalyst and hydrogen, need a closer look.

Reduction

CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O is exothermic: too much H₂ sinters fresh catalyst.

HZ-11

Operation

Beds above ~300 °C run away; CO forms metal carbonyls.

HZ-10, HZ-04

Unloading

Reduced copper glows in air; inert entry under N₂ asphyxiates.

HZ-12, HZ-13

Major accident hazards

Eleven rows could cause multiple fatalities

The matrix ranks every row. A few rows matter more than any rank: the major accidents.

MAH: an event that could cause multiple fatalities, major damage or a major environmental release (ISO 17776). Sorted by risk.

Interactive · model run, LIC-204 forced open, trips off

Blow-by: how long before the boot runs dry?

Model limit: once the boot is empty the model passes no gas through LV-204.

Fully open, the boot empties under 3 min after the low alarm: a person cannot close it in time, so LSLL-204 and XV-204 are proposed.

Interactive · model runs (process-safety-time study)

Purge blocked: the trip comes within three minutes

The make-up source limits the pressure; with a 93 bar a source only PSHH-203 (2.2 min) keeps the loop below the 88 bar g relief set point.

Interactive · model runs, operator ESD then HV-205

External fire: can HV-205 halve the pressure in 15 min?

Rule (API 521, typical): reach 50 % of design pressure in 15 min. Design pressure taken as the PSV-201 set point, 88 bar g (assumed).

Wide open, HV-205 reaches 44 bar g in 5.1 min; at 25 % it needs 19.7 min, so the valve must open fully and remotely.

Actions

Seven additions close the gaps the register found

The model runs showed where people are too slow. These additions answer them.

Same names in the HAZOP, Safety layers and LOPA tabs. 2oo3: two out of three sensors must agree; 1oo2: either of two valves shuts.

Interactive · from the tab's section 7

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Eleven hazards are MAHs; blow-by and fires rank highest.
  • LV-204 wide open empties the boot 167 s after its alarm.
  • HV-205 halves the pressure in 5.1 min; at 25 % it fails.
  • Seven additions and five studies carry the register on.
