Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Needed PFD = credited PFD ÷ the LOPA RRF.
  • I-1 to I-4 need SIL 1 or 2; each achieves only SIL 1.
  • XV-202 sets most of each PFD: XV-202B is the common fix.
  • Proof test yearly, de-energise to trip, reset only when clear.

This deck

Key Takeaways

1

Write the SRS from the LOPA result before building any logic.

2

Name the position of every device in the safe state, running ones too.

3

Measure the PST on the model; keep the response under half of it.

4

Verify each PFD against what its scenario needs, not a generic SIL.

5

Look for the shared component when every SIF misses its target.
