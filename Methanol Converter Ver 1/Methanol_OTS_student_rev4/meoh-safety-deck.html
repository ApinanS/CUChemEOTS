Part 1 · The six layers

Why is the loop protected by six layers, not one?

Part 1 · the plan

The principle

Protection in rings around the hazard

Equipment fails, control fails, people fail, and sometimes two at once.

So each ring must work independently of the one inside it.

The outer rings act only when the inner ones were not enough: strength in reserve.

Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Six layers, each working if the inner ones fail.
  • BPCS, alarms and SIS need power; relief does not.
  • The register holds 78 items; 10 are recommended.
  • Fail positions make power or air loss safe; shared sensors do not.

The idea

Every key variable gets a ladder of limits

Green: safe operation, held by the BPCS.
Orange: a sustained excursion; alarm, then trip.
Red: beyond the critical limit, damage comes quickly.

Bed temperature shown; critical 320 °C is an assumed teaching value (Safety layers tab).

How low can circulation go?

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • An IOW stacks target, alarm, trip, relief and critical limit.
  • On the model, alarm to trip takes minutes.
  • V-201 low level has no trip: the boot empties instead.
  • Low circulation overheats the beds long before FSLL-201 acts.

Layer 3 · principles

A trip removes the energy source

Independent

Own sensors, own logic solver SIS-201, own valve XV-202.

Fail safe

De-energise to trip: power loss closes XV-202.

Latched

Manual reset only, then start permissives.

Here

Make-up gas off; C-201 keeps sweeping heat out.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Every trip closes XV-202; only I-3 and ESD stop C-201.
  • XV-202 alone limits TSHH to SIL 1; XV-202B lifts it.
  • FT-201 sharing and no low-level trip are the gaps.
  • PSV-201 needs no power; supply pressure keeps it unchallenged.

Key Takeaways

1

Give every layer its own sensor, logic and final element.

2

Choose fail positions so that losing power or air stops the hazard.

3

Space alarm, trip and critical limits by the time each layer needs.

4

Vote sensors and double the final element when one valve sets the SIL.

5

Size relief for the worst case and keep its path to flare open.
