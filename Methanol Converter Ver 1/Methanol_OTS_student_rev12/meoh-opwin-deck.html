Operability

Four things shape the operating window

The operating window is the set of steady states where every controlled variable sits at its set point and no limit is broken. Operability means the plant reaches that range safely and profitably.

Bounds

Hard, soft and capacity bounds

Hard

Must never be crossed. Hottest bed 300 °C (TSHH), loop 85 bar g (PSHH-203), circulation 868 (FSLL-201), level 90 %.

Soft

Crossable briefly; something degrades. Catalyst 285 °C, loop CH₄ 13 %, separator 45 °C, purge 150.

Capacity

End of a handle's travel. A valve at 5 or 95 %, C-201 at 95 % speed, can no longer hold its variable.

What part 3 settled

Key Takeaways

1

List the variability first; size the window for it, not for the design point.

2

Classify every bound as hard, soft or capacity, and rank it on the IOW.

3

Take the overlap of the elements: the loop turns down only 1.47 : 1.

4

Read each window edge for its cause, then add the handle that moves it.
