After assembly · MEOH-V1-PM-COM-001 §5

Tightness below the relief set

N₂/He leak test

In steps 10, 50, 100 % to 75 bar g; every flange sniffed; 1 h hold with no decay.

Below 0.9 × set

75 bar g stays under 0.9 × 88 = 79.2 bar g, so PSV-201 does not simmer.

PSV bench test

Set and seat tested on a bench (API 527), installed only after the hydrotest.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • FAT proves the logic at the works; SAT proves it end to end.
  • On the model copy every trip acts 5.25 s after its input.
  • Hydrotest at 1.3 or 1.5 × design pressure.
  • Leak-test with N₂/He below 90 % of the PSV set.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Dry, purge, load and reduce before any process gas.
  • Fail positions must match the P&ID, the instrument list and the model.
  • CS-04 to CS-10 are the simulator's open procedures.
  • Hold points gate each phase; queries close before ordering.

Order the long-lead converter first; it sets mechanical completion.
