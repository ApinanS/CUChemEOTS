How fast does C-201 power rise with speed?

From design to full speed flow rises 25 % but power almost doubles, 6.53 → 12.59 MW: loop pressure drop grows with flow squared.

Interactive · all four tests, size by size

Which is the smallest size that passes?

C-201 needs 3 × 300 mm² for current; the pump's 16 mm² starts with 14.7 % drop against 15 %, so a longer route needs a bigger size.

Interactive · C-201 feeder, 3 × 300 mm², 25 kA board

What does starting current do to the board?

The cable drop stays tiny; the board dip, Istart/Ik, passes 15 % above about 4.5 × FLC: a direct-on-line start would sag the whole 11 kV board.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • C-201 draws 842 A; the cable is sized for 1052 A.
  • Derating to 0.787 calls for three runs of 300 mm².
  • Long low-voltage feeders are set by starting drop.
  • A direct-on-line start would dip the board 20 %.

How often it leaks decides the zone

Every motor and cable of part 2 stands somewhere on the plot. Is that place hazardous?

Continuous → Zone 0

Released all the time; none on this loop.

Primary → Zone 1

Released in normal operation: seal vents, sample points.

Secondary → Zone 2

Released only in a fault: flanges, valve stems.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Grade sets zone: primary → Zone 1, secondary → Zone 2.

  • Equipment is marked IIC T3, EPL Gb or Gc.

Key Takeaways

1

Rate a machine's driver on its largest absorbed power, plus margin.

2

Sum demand with demand factors, then size the transformer with margin.

3

Size a cable on current, voltage drop and short circuit together.

4

Classify from release grade, then pick group and T-class from the gas.
