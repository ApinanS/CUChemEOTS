Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • The loop loses 5.6 bar at design; the beds take 2.33.
  • C-201: r = 1.074, ΔT = 8.6 K, 6528 kW (hand check 6200).

  • Zone A 88 bar g, zone B 100 bar g; circulation margin 13.6 %.

Key Takeaways

1

Size every line and machine on the worst case, not the normal one.

2

Close mass, component and element balances before issuing an HMB.

3

Check velocity, ρv² and ΔP together; high-pressure gas fails on velocity first.

4

Expect circulator power to rise with the cube of flow.

5

Raise a query when a drawn size or design value fails the check.
