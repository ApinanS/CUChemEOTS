What does closing it do to the loop?

Isolations of the circulator, let-down or purge trip the loop within minutes; the gauge-glass valves change only what the operator sees.

Interactive · the same valve reopened, 10 min more

Reopening is an operation too

Reopening the make-up block with FIC-202 wound up at 100 % in AUTO drives a bed to the 300 °C trip: put the controller in MAN first.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • A dial is read to half a minor division.
  • Gauge and DCS differ by the process between their tappings.
  • Eleven valves are normally open; five are sealed or locked.
  • Closing or reopening a block valve can trip the loop.

The sheet checks answers. Training exercises check what was actually done.

Tasks and questions weigh equally: walking without answering, or answering without walking, caps the grade.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Identification passes at 90 % of the 68 round tags.
  • The rounds sheet is checked against the live plant.
  • Training field checks tick from log lines, scored out of 5.
  • VP exercises score tasks and answers together, out of 10.

Key Takeaways

1

Walk the round in order and climb each platform once.

2

Read a gauge to half a division and compare it with the board.

3

Know every hand valve's normal position before you touch it.

4

Put the controller in MAN before reopening its block valve.

5

Record every reading: the log is the evidence of the round.
