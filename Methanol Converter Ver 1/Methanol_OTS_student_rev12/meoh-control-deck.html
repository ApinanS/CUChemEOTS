Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • A 5 % step gives each handle its KKK, τ\tauτ and θ\thetaθ.
  • Quench and bypass act in seconds; the purge needs minutes.
  • Make-up sets the plant rate, so the purge holds pressure.
  • Quench shots and E-201 couple the temperature loops.
