      Avon on R-201: alarm, trip, damage

      Avon's quench was wide open and the bed still ran. On R-201 the TSHH-2xx trip closes XV-202.

      The alarm leaves about two minutes to the 300 °C trip; bypassed, the catalyst reaches 310 °C in nine minutes.

      Interactive · model runs · LV-204 stuck open, no operator action

      Grangemouth on R-201: how fast does V-201 empty?

      Normal opening ≈ 50 %. Below the boot, 75 bar g gas meets the 6 bar g let-down drum.

      Wide open, the boot is empty under six minutes after a stuck valve, and nothing trips: hence R02 and R03.

      Patterns no trip can stop · 1 of 2

      Hot hydrogen, flanges and leaks

      Anacortes 2010

      HTHA cracked carbon steel. R-201 outlet ≈ 268 °C: R18 checks API 941.

      Gonfreville 2006

      Flange leaked on restart. E-201 hot side: R17 controlled bolting.

      Gallatin 2011

      Hydrogen leak, no detector. Loop gas ≈ 82 % H₂: R17 adds detection.

      API 941 = the Nelson curves for steels in hot hydrogen. R-numbers are HAZOP recommendations.

      Patterns no trip can stop · 2 of 2

      Start-up, entry and hot work

      Moerdijk 2014

      Fresh catalyst ran away on reheat. R20 reduction hold points; R22 slow make-up.

      Delaware City 2005

      Nitrogen at an open manway killed two. R21 entry rules; never rescue alone.

      Bethune Point 2006

      Torch sparks over a methanol vent. Hot-work permit and gas test.

      Part 3 · summary

      What part 3 settled

      • Each case maps to a place on the loop.
      • The circulation-cut runaway: about two minutes from alarm to trip.
      • V-201 empties in minutes with no trip: R02, R03.
      • Materials, bolting, entry and permits need procedures, not trips.
