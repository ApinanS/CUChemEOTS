Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

Derivation · two numbers per SIF

The needed PFD comes straight from LOPA

A trip that is fast enough must also work when demanded.

Independence, SIF by SIF

Mode of operation (IEC 61511)

One SIF is demanded more than once a year

Demand rate = initiating events × the BPCS, standby and alarm layers credited in front (LOPA tab). Above 1 /yr: verify a failure rate per hour, not PFDavg.

What part 3 settled

Key Takeaways

1

Write the SRS from the LOPA result before building any logic.

2

Name the element that removes the hazard, proven on the model.

3

Measure the PST per demand; keep the response under half.

4

Write the coast-down into the safe state of a compressor.

5

Give each trip its own sensor, or LOPA will not credit it.
