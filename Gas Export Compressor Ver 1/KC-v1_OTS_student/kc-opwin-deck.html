Part 1 · summary

What part 1 settled

  • Window: set points held, no bound crossed.
  • No flow set point: PIC-101 makes the speed follow the wells.
  • Five sources move the point: wells, air, arrival, gas, cooling.
  • Alone: cooling valves open wide at 35 °C medium; PI-112 alarms at 80 barg arrival.

Part 2 · summary

What part 2 settled

  • Nineteen bounds of six kinds, reported root cause first.
  • Surge margin 61 % at design; the recycle opens below 68 % flow at full speed.
  • At 30 °C the pipeline sets the top (106 %); above 40 °C the GT does.
  • Window 10–106 %; without recycle 55–105 %.

Part 3 · summary

What part 3 settled

  • Type I: 6 of 195 hand settings inside at 100 %, a diagonal band.
  • The reachable CV set is a curve, not a rectangle.
  • Type III windows are bands of production; no holes in five grids.
  • Monte Carlo: 93 % inside around 95 % production.

Key Takeaways

1

Write the variability down first; the window is sized for that list.

2

Report violated bounds root cause first: driver, then pressures, then set points.

3

Check the driver at the hottest afternoon, not at ISO conditions.

4

Read the train as a series plant; the recycle buys turndown with energy.

5

Check the inside of the window and how often the plant is in it.
